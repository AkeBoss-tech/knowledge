# Co-Scientist Workflow Brief

## Goal

Demonstrate a human-supervised, evidence-first hypothesis-development loop
inspired by Google's Co-Scientist: generate diverse candidates; critique and
debate them; rank a tournament; evolve promising candidates; then produce a
meta-review for a human decision.

## Demonstration objective

For a fictional edge-service workload, what mechanism could reduce energy per
request without violating a fixed latency service-level objective?

This is a workflow fixture. Its seed evidence is illustrative only, and no
recommendation or experimental result is asserted.

## Operating constraints

- Every candidate must link to recorded source evidence or be marked unsupported.
- Score plausibility, novelty, testability, safety, and goal alignment separately.
- Preserve diverse mechanism families; do not let near-duplicates dominate the tournament.
- A human reviewer must approve the final hypothesis and any experiment.

## Open questions

- Which domain-specific evaluation tool can measure the desired outcome safely?
- What evidence would falsify each finalist?
- Which hypotheses are too close to existing approaches to count as novel?
