# Current Plan

## Objective

Run a bounded, human-supervised idea tournament for the demonstration objective
in `topics/brief.md`. Produce candidates, a transparent scorecard, a
diversity-aware shortlist, and a meta-review; do not represent output as a
scientific finding.

## Execution contract

1. Supervisor reads constraints, sources, prior candidates, and human feedback.
2. Generation proposes distinct, falsifiable hypotheses with evidence links and
   disconfirming observations.
3. Critic performs reflection and adversarial debate; it records contradictions
   and safety/feasibility gaps rather than silently fixing them.
4. Ranking runs a documented tournament over alignment, plausibility, novelty,
   testability, safety, and evidence coverage.
5. Evolution revises only the strongest, non-blocked candidates, preserving
   original proposals and review history.
6. Proximity clustering prevents near-duplicates from consuming the shortlist.
7. Meta-review creates a decision-ready roadmap. A human accepts, rejects, or
   asks for another iteration.

## Exit conditions

- all proposed claims trace to evidence or are labeled unsupported
- finalists include a proposed falsifier and evaluation plan
- contradictions, duplicates, and uncertainty remain in the ledgers
- a human approval precedes experimentation or publication
